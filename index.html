Frequently asked questions

Is CueLoop a habit tracker?

Not really — CueLoop is a recurring reminder and cue app rather than a streak tracker. It focuses on timely, gentle nudges instead of charts, social feeds or heavy routine planning.

Can it remind me to drink water, fix my posture or take eye breaks?

Yes. Create a cue loop for each habit, set its cadence and active hours, and CueLoop nudges you with a gentle sound, a soft buzz or a screen flash. Hydration, posture and 20-20-20 eye breaks are some of the most popular uses.

Does CueLoop work with Wear OS watches?

Yes. With a Wear OS watch (Pixel Watch, Galaxy Watch and others) your cues can mirror to your wrist as notifications — great when the phone is silent, in your pocket or across the room.

Will the cues arrive on time?

CueLoop uses Android exact alarms and a foreground service so cues stay close to schedule without drifting. Some devices need notifications, exact-alarm permission (Android 12+) or battery-optimization exclusions enabled — CueLoop links you straight to those settings.

Does CueLoop require an account?

No. CueLoop is offline-first. It does not need account creation or cloud sync for its reminder features, and there is no tracking or analytics in the core experience.

Is CueLoop subscription based?

No. CueLoop has a free tier with all core features and a one-time CueLoop Pro unlock that adds the full sound set, custom audio files and extra scheduling features. A Weekly Free Day also unlocks all Pro features one day each week.

Can I use it as a gentler Pomodoro or interval timer?

Yes. Set a focus/break cadence and CueLoop nudges you with a soft cue instead of locking you out or blaring an alarm — a calmer alternative to strict productivity timers.
