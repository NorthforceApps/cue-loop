Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

CueLoop is an offline-first Android app that runs recurring reminder cues on your device and, when supported, mirrors cue notifications to a Wear OS watch. Its core features work without an account or sign-in. This policy explains what data is processed, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • CueLoop does not require account creation.
  • Reminder data is stored locally on your device.
  • There is no tracking or analytics in the core experience.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play Billing.
  • The public CueLoop website may use Google Analytics to understand page visits and improve the site.
  • If Firebase is enabled in a build, crash diagnostics and limited analytics may be collected (see Section 6).

2. Information We Process

Reminder data stored on your device: cue names, intervals and cadence, active-hours windows, cue mode (sound, vibration, screen flash), chosen sounds, theme/preference flags, and purchase entitlement state. This is stored locally so the app can run your configured cue loops and restore your preferences.

Notifications & alarms: CueLoop uses notifications, exact-alarm scheduling, and a foreground service so cues arrive on time. On some devices it offers guidance to enable notifications, exact alarms (Android 12+) or battery-optimization exclusions.

Wear OS mirroring: if you use Wear OS mirroring, reminder notifications are relayed through standard Android notification mechanisms to compatible wearable devices.

3. Permissions Used

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show cue and status notifications (Android 13+).
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM — deliver cues on time (Android 12+).
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — keep cue playback (sound/buzz/flash) reliable.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — declares the media-playback foreground-service type used to play cue sounds reliably from the background.
  • VIBRATE — haptic (buzz) cues.
  • WAKE_LOCK — keep cue delivery reliable while active.
  • READ access for custom audio — only if you choose your own sound file (Pro).

4. In-App Purchases

CueLoop offers an optional one-time Pro unlock via Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google; we do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase tokens/entitlement state are stored to unlock purchased features. A Weekly Free Day also unlocks Pro features one day each week at no cost.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers needed to run platform functionality, such as Google Play Billing (for purchases), Firebase services (if enabled in the build, described below), and Google Analytics for public website measurement.

6. Firebase Crash Diagnostics and Analytics (if enabled)

Some builds may enable Firebase. When enabled, Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics may process limited technical data such as crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and basic interaction events used for product quality. We do not intentionally send your reminder content or payment card data to Firebase. See Firebase privacy and Google privacy.

7. Data Deletion

CueLoop does not collect or store your personal data on our own servers. All app data is stored locally on your device. To remove it, clear the app's storage/data from Android settings, or delete/uninstall the app.

8. Website Analytics

The public CueLoop website may use Google Analytics to measure page visits, referrers, approximate geography, browser/device information, and interactions with the site. This helps us understand whether the website and guides are useful. Website analytics do not include reminder content or payment card data. See Google privacy.

9. Children's Privacy

CueLoop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: northforceapps@protonmail.com
