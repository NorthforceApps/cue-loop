Desk break reminder app — hydration, posture & 20-20-20 eye breaks

If you sit at a screen all day, a simple desk break reminder app can do a lot: a nudge to drink water, a prompt to fix your posture, and a cue to rest your eyes with the 20-20-20 rule. CueLoop runs all three as gentle recurring cues on your own cadence — a soft sound, a buzz or a quiet screen flash — with no account and no logging.

One app, three desk habits

Create a separate cue loop for each habit and set each to its own rhythm:

  • Hydration reminder — a sip-water nudge every 30–60 minutes.
  • Posture reminder — a "sit up, drop your shoulders" cue every 20–30 minutes.
  • Eye breaks (20-20-20) — every 20 minutes, look ~20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds.

What is the 20-20-20 rule?

It's a common eye-strain tip for screen work: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for 20 seconds to relax your focusing muscles. It's easy to forget mid-task — which is exactly what a recurring cue is for. Set a cue loop to a 20-minute interval and CueLoop reminds you, gently, every time.

Set it up in under a minute

  1. Tap to add a cue loop and name it (e.g. "Water", "Posture", "Eyes").
  2. Pick a mode — vibration or a screen flash keeps it silent at a shared desk.
  3. Set the interval and an active-hours window so cues stop after work.
  4. Use the instant Test button to feel the cue, then start now.

Calmer than a gamified tracker

Apps like WaterMinder or Plant Nanny turn hydration into logging and streaks, which works for some people but adds friction and an account. CueLoop takes the opposite approach: no intake logging, no account, no guilt — just a well-timed nudge you can act on and forget. The same goes for posture and eye breaks, all in one place.

Reliable, on-time cues

CueLoop uses Android exact alarms plus a foreground service so desk cues stay close to schedule. On some devices you may need to allow notifications, grant exact-alarm permission (Android 12+), or exclude the app from battery optimization — CueLoop links you straight to those settings.

Frequently asked questions

Can one app cover water, posture and eye breaks?

Yes. Make a separate cue loop for each — a sip-water nudge, a posture reset and a 20-20-20 eye break — each on its own cadence and mode.

What is the 20-20-20 rule?

Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds to rest your eyes. Set a cue loop to 20 minutes and CueLoop reminds you.

How is this different from a water-tracking app?

Gamified trackers such as WaterMinder or Plant Nanny ask you to log intake and grow streaks. CueLoop just gives you a calm, well-timed nudge — no logging, no account, no guilt.

Can the reminders stay silent at work?

Yes. Use vibration or a brief screen flash instead of sound, so a desk cue does not disturb the room.
