CueLoop guides

Practical how-tos for building gentle, on-time reminder routines with CueLoop.

Wear OS reminder app — recurring nudges on your wrist

Get gentle recurring reminders right on your Wear OS watch. CueLoop mirrors cues to a Pixel Watch, Galaxy Watch and more, so a vibration on your wrist keeps you on track when the phone is away — a simple recurring reminder app for Wear OS.

Desk break reminder app — hydration, posture & 20-20-20 eye breaks

A simple desk break reminder for office and remote work: CueLoop nudges you to drink water, fix your posture and rest your eyes with the 20-20-20 rule. A calm, no-account alternative to gamified trackers like WaterMinder or Plant Nanny.

A gentle Pomodoro alternative — focus cues without the lockout

Want focus sprints without a harsh timer or app blocker? CueLoop nudges you between work and breaks with a soft sound, buzz or flash — a calmer alternative to strict Pomodoro timers and focus apps like Forest.

ADHD reminder app — gentle nudges for time blindness

Lose track of time? CueLoop gives steady, recurring nudges — a sound, a buzz or a screen flash on your phone and Wear OS watch — to anchor the day and ease time blindness. A calm, low-friction ADHD-friendly reminder app.

Posture, eye break & breathing reminder app — tiny resets every 10 minutes

Use CueLoop as a simple recurring reminder for posture, eye breaks and deep breathing. Set a 10-minute cue loop for small body and focus resets during screen work, without streaks, logging or a noisy alarm.

Memory repetition reminder app — timed cues for recall practice

Want to strengthen memory with short recall sessions? CueLoop can run timed repetition cues every 30 minutes or on your own cadence, so you remember to review a fact, phrase, name or idea during the day.
